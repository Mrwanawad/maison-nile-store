# Frequently asked questions

### How long does delivery take?
{delivery_min}-{delivery_max} working days to any governorate.

### Can I pay cash on delivery?
Yes. You pay the courier when your order arrives. We'll call you to confirm the order first.

### Can I pay online?
When online payment is available you'll see it at checkout. Payments are processed securely by Paymob; we never see your card details.

### How do I track my order?
Use the [order tracking page](/track) with your order number and mobile number.

### Can I change or cancel my order?
Message us as soon as possible on WhatsApp. If it hasn't shipped yet, we can change or cancel it.

### What if the size doesn't fit?
You can exchange it within 14 days. See [delivery & exchanges](/pages/shipping-returns).
